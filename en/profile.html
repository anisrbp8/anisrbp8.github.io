Profile

Research, teaching and academic leadership

I am an Associate Professor in Computer Science and Engineering at Université Paris 8, a member of the CHArt laboratory (THIM team) and Section 61 of the French National Council of Universities (CNU). Since 2020, I have directed the MIASHS master’s programme and headed the Ingénierie – Cognition – Handicap department. I am responsible for the Technology and Disability track.

My research focuses on digital accessibility, signal and image processing, and assistive technologies. I use artificial intelligence methods and work on brain–computer interfaces and human–computer interaction.
