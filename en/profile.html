Academic profile

From early R&D work in signal and image processing to current leadership responsibilities at Université Paris 8.

Profile

Continuity between technology, real-world use and inclusion

I am an Associate Professor in Computer Science and Engineering at Université Paris 8, a member of the CHArt laboratory (THIM team) and of France’s CNU Section 61. Since 2021, I have headed the Ingénierie – Cognition – Handicap Department and directed the MIASHS Master's Programme and its Technology and Disability track.

My activities combine applied research, teaching, academic leadership and international cooperation. My work draws on artificial intelligence, signal and image processing, computer vision and brain-computer interfaces.
