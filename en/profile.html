Profile

Continuity between technology, real-world use and inclusion

Anis Rojbi is an Associate Professor in Computer Science and Engineering at Université Paris 8, a member of the CHArt laboratory (THIM team) and of France’s CNU Section 61. Since 2021, he has headed the Ingénierie – Cognition – Handicap Department and directed the MIASHS Master's Programme and its Technology and Disability track.

His activities combine applied research, teaching, academic leadership and international cooperation. His work draws on artificial intelligence, signal and image processing, computer vision and brain-computer interfaces.
