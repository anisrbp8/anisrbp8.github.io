Anis RojbiAssociate Professor in Computer Science and Engineering

I study how images, interfaces and artificial intelligence models can be adapted to their users’ abilities. My research combines digital accessibility, signal processing and human–computer interaction.

Research in focusImages and accessibility

Exploring an image at different levels of detail

An adapted representation can change how an image is explored. In this work, graphic and haptic granularity can be customised for touchscreen exploration by sighted or blind users.

Research questions

01
Images and accessibility

How can visual information be adapted to a user’s perceptual abilities?

I study multi-level visual, interactive and tactile representations to make images and digital documents more accessible.

Methods and work in area 1
02
Multimodal analysis

Can fatigue be detected by combining vision and physiological data?

This work combines visual cues and physiological measures to study fatigue using machine learning.

Methods and work in area 2
03
BCI and EEG

How can models become more robust when EEG data are limited?

I explore natural and synthetic data augmentation for physical-action classification using deep learning.
