Accessibility

Accessibility statement and design principles

The site is designed with WCAG 2.2 AA and RGAA objectives, without claiming full conformance before a comprehensive human audit.

A

Built-in principles

  • semantic HTML and coherent heading hierarchy;
  • keyboard navigation and visible focus;
  • skip link to main content;
  • strong contrast and responsive layout;
  • charts accompanied by text values or tables;
  • support for prefers-reduced-motion;
  • keyboard-accessible data tables;
  • complete French and English page sets with explicit language links.

Conformance status

This version targets WCAG 2.2 AA and RGAA requirements, but full conformance requires a criterion-by-criterion human audit across assistive technologies and browsers.
