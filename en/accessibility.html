Accessibility

Accessibility statement

This statement records the findings of the review of my academic website, describes barriers that remain and explains how to request an accessible format.

Compliance status

Anis Rojbi’s personal academic website is non-compliant with the French accessibility framework RGAA 4.1.2. Outstanding screen reader checks and documented barriers prevent a final compliance percentage from being reported.

Assessment results

All 106 criteria were reviewed across 29 pages on 22 November 2026. Each criterion is counted once for the site. A failure on any page means that the criterion fails for the site.

  • 49 criteria met on every relevant page
  • 4 criteria with at least one failure
  • 37 criteria that do not apply to the site
  • 16 criteria with checks still outstanding

Download the results for all 29 pages and 106 criteria (CSV). The file uses C for met, NC for failed, NA for not applicable and NT for incomplete.

Known barriers

  • Scientific documents (13.3). The Pattaya paper contains tables whose structure is poorly represented in the PDF. The IFRATH proceedings and the two 2017 papers have insufficient tagging. The server hosting the CORESA paper refused the download. The entries for the 2017 papers link to their full text in HTML and include descriptions of the figures. Full accessible equivalents for these documents have yet to be verified.
  • Contact without JavaScript. The return link on Formspree’s generic thank-you pages relies on a click and JavaScript. You can return using your browser’s Back command. With JavaScript enabled, error messages and confirmation appear on the contact page itself.

The four CV and biography PDFs were also checked with veraPDF and reviewed visually. Their HTML versions contain the same information. This review claims no disproportionate-burden or document exemptions.

Scope and method

The self-assessment covers the public website in French and English, including the 404 page. The technologies used are HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SVG and PDF. The review on GitHub and test logs document the source and browser checks.

HTML checks

The footer links to the Nu HTML checker for the page you are reading. The W3C HTML5 logo is used under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence. A WCAG conformance logo is a claim by the publisher for every criterion at the stated level. The evidence does not support a site-wide WCAG A, AA or AAA claim.

Report a barrier

You can report a barrier or request an accessible format. Please identify the page or document and describe the difficulty. You can also read the full online CV and online biography.

Further assistance

If you have reported a barrier and still need assistance, you may contact France’s Defender of Rights.
