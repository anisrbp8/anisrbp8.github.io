Research

Assistive technology, AI and adaptive systems

Three applied research areas focused on content accessibility, multimodal analysis and brain-computer interfaces.

02

Area 1

Making images and documents more accessible

A multi-granular approach transforms visual content into several levels of information that can be used visually, interactively or through tactile representations.

Source image
Variable granulation
Granule decomposition & relationships
Selection / interpolation
Visual or tactile output
Application. Web and digital document access through alternative, multi-level and tactile representations.

Area 2

Detecting fatigue from multimodal data

The methods combine computer vision, machine learning and physiological measures to extract complementary indicators.

Visual cues

  • facial expressions
  • eye movements
  • behavioural cues associated with fatigue

Physiological measures

  • heart rate
  • blood pressure
  • oxygen saturation
