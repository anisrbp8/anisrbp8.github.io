Anis Rojbi

Biography

Academic background and research areas

I am an Associate Professor (Maître de conférences) in Computer Science and Engineering at Université Paris 8, a member of the CHArt research unit (THIM team) and Section 61 of the French National Council of Universities (CNU). My research focuses on digital accessibility, signal and image processing, and assistive technologies. Two questions run through my work: how people can explore an image by touch, and how machine learning can help interpret physiological signals. Both inform my research on brain–computer interfaces and human–computer interaction.

Since 2020, I have directed the MIASHS master’s programme and headed the Ingénierie – Cognition – Handicap (ICH) department. I am responsible for the Technology and Disability track. I coordinate Erasmus+ exchanges with Kyiv and Sfax. I also worked with colleagues in Tunis on a Hubert Curien Partnership to adapt digital documents for reading by touch.
