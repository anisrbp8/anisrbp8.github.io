Professional biography of Anis Rojbi.

Biography

Anis Rojbi is an Associate Professor (Maître de conférences) in Computer Science and Engineering at Université Paris 8, a member of the CHArt research unit, THIM team, and France's CNU Section 61. His research focuses on assistive technologies, digital accessibility and human-computer interaction, drawing on artificial intelligence, signal and image processing, computer vision and brain-computer interfaces. Since 2021, he has headed the Ingénierie - Cognition - Handicap department and directed the MIASHS master's programme and its Technology and Disability track. He also coordinates Erasmus+ and PHC collaborations with international partners, particularly in Ukraine and Tunisia, spanning education, academic mobility and inclusive technologies.
