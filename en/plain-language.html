My work in plain language

Who am I?

My name is Anis Rojbi. I teach and do research at Université Paris 8 in France. I look at ways to make digital tools more accessible to people with disabilities.

My research

A raised map can be read with your fingers. I study how to turn a digital map into one that people can explore by touch. I also study signs of fatigue and brain signals associated with certain physical actions.

My teaching

I teach students how to build accessible tools and websites. Students also work on projects for non-profit organisations.

My partnerships

I organise exchanges and projects with universities in other countries. For example, Paris 8 runs exchanges with universities in Kyiv, Ukraine, and Sfax, Tunisia. Read about these exchanges.
