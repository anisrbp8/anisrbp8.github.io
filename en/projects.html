Coordinated projects and programmes

The Erasmus+ partnerships are ongoing. The E-Tactile research project ended in 2024.

Erasmus+ ICMUkraineCoordinator

Kyiv Polytechnic Institute

Since 2022 I have coordinated Erasmus+ exchanges for students and staff at Paris 8 and Kyiv Polytechnic Institute.

These exchanges are part of the university’s international mobility programmes. My CV gives the funding figures for the university as a whole.

University of Sfax

I coordinate academic exchanges and joint teaching and research with the University of Sfax. With our partners, we are preparing a double-degree programme with the École nationale d’ingénieurs de Sfax (ENIS).
