Home / Privacy

Privacy

Minimal data and no advertising tracking

The site intentionally includes no advertising, profiling or publicly exposed receiving email address in its code.

Contact

The contact page lets you send a message using Formspree. The information you enter — name, email address, optional organisation, subject and message — is sent to Formspree so that your enquiry can be delivered. The receiving email address is not published in the website code. Please do not include sensitive information that is not necessary for your enquiry.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub describes its processing of technical data in its privacy statement.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not include third-party advertising, profiling or analytics tools.

External links

When you follow an external link, the destination website’s privacy policy applies.

Reading preferences

If you change the reading preferences, your choices are stored only in this browser so they remain available on other pages. They are not sent to a server. The reset button removes them.
