Privacy

Privacy and your data

This website presents my academic work. It contains no advertising or profiling tools.

Contact

The contact page lets you send me a message using Formspree. The information you enter — name, email address, optional organisation, subject and message — is sent to this service to deliver your enquiry. See the Formspree privacy policy for details of its data processing. Please avoid sensitive information that is not needed for your enquiry.

With JavaScript enabled, the form displays the response on this website. Without JavaScript, Formspree can display its own response page. Its standard thank-you pages contain Google analytics and advertising scripts that require JavaScript to run.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub describes its processing of technical data in its privacy statement.

Cookies and analytics

These pages load no analytics or advertising scripts. Reading preferences use the browser’s local storage.

External links

Figures and full-size images are hosted on this site. When you follow a link to a publisher, university or research profile, the destination site’s privacy policy applies.

Reading preferences

Reading preferences are stored locally in your browser so they remain available as you move between pages. They are not sent to the website’s server. The reset button clears them.
