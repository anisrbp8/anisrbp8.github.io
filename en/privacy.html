Privacy

Privacy and your data

This website presents my academic work. It contains no advertising or profiling tools.

When you write to me

I use your name, email address, subject and message to answer questions about research, teaching and professional collaborations. Giving your organisation’s name is optional. Please leave out health information and other sensitive details. For applications and student records, use the university’s official services.

How your message reaches me

The form sends your details to Formspree, Inc., which forwards them to my mailbox. Formspree hosts its service in the United States and provides a submission history in the recipient’s account. Formspree states that it relies on standard contractual clauses when acting as a processor. See its privacy policy and hosting information.

With JavaScript enabled, the confirmation appears on this site. Without JavaScript, your browser opens Formspree’s response page, which is covered by the provider’s own practices.

Your rights

To request access, correction, erasure or restriction, to object to processing or to request portability where applicable, write to me. You can also use the postal address in the legal notice or lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL).

Site visits and reading preferences

GitHub Pages logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub explains how it handles these data in its privacy statement.

The pages on this site load no advertising or analytics scripts. Your browser stores your colour, text size and spacing preferences. The reset button clears them. Figures are hosted here. When you follow an external link, the destination site’s privacy policy applies.
