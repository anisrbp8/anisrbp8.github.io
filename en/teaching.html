MIASHS Master’s programme

Teaching

I teach mainly in the MIASHS Master’s programme, a multidisciplinary course bringing together computing, health and the humanities.

Courses and projects

Main teaching areas

Teaching combines technical foundations, digital accessibility, prototyping and projects carried out with external partners.

M1
Web

Accessible Front-End Development

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive design and accessibility principles applied to concrete projects.

M1–M2
IoT

Electronics and Internet of Things

Prototyping, sensors, connected devices and the implementation of technical solutions shaped by user needs.

M1–M2
Cloud

Networks and cloud infrastructures

Network architectures, cloud services and the infrastructure required for digital applications.

Project
fieldwork

Technical projects, internships and work-study

Supervision of projects with partner organisations, internship follow-up and support for apprenticeship assignments.

Teaching approach: real disability-related situations, teamwork, prototyping, autonomy and materials adapted to multidisciplinary cohorts.

Teaching impact

Projects with non-profit and partner organisations

Each year, approximately 20 non-profit organisations in France and abroad benefit from an accessible website project delivered by students.

From needs to delivery

  1. identify an organisation and its needs
  2. formalise requirements
  3. design and develop accessible websites
  4. test, document and deliver

Work-study and employability

Work-study coordinator for approximately 10 to 15 students per year: employer relations, application support, approval of apprenticeship assignments and coordination with the university team.
