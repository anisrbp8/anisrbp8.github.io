MIASHS Master’s programme

Teaching

I teach accessible web development, electronics and networks on the MIASHS master’s programme at Paris 8. I also supervise student projects with non-profit organisations.

For prospective and current students

Technology and Disability master’s

The Technology and Disability track (Technologie et Handicap) is part of the MIASHS master’s programme at Université Paris 8. Students learn to design and evaluate digital tools for people with disabilities. Apprenticeships are available in the second year (M2).

Courses and projects

Main teaching areas

My teaching combines computing fundamentals, digital accessibility, prototyping and projects developed with external partners.

M1
Web

Accessible Front-End Development

Students build pages in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, then check how they work on small screens and with a keyboard.

M1–M2
IoT

Electronics and Internet of Things

Students use sensors to build and test prototypes of connected devices.

M1–M2
Cloud

Networks and cloud infrastructures

The course examines the networks and cloud services that applications depend on.

Project
fieldwork

Technical projects, internships and work-study

Supervision of projects with partner organisations, internship follow-up and support for apprenticeship assignments.

Teaching approach: real disability-related situations, teamwork, prototyping, autonomy and materials adapted to multidisciplinary cohorts.

Student projects

Projects with non-profit and partner organisations

Each year, students develop accessible websites for around 20 non-profit organisations.

From needs to delivery

  1. identify an organisation and its needs
  2. formalise requirements
  3. design and develop accessible websites
  4. test, document and deliver

Work-study and employability

In the second year, I help students prepare applications and agree the scope of their placements with employers and the university apprenticeship office.
