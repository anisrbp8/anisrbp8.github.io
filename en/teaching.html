MIASHS Master’s programme

Teaching

I teach mainly on the MIASHS master’s programme, which combines computing, disability and new technologies.

For prospective and current students

Technology and Disability master’s

The Technology and Disability track (Technologie et Handicap) is part of the MIASHS master’s programme at Université Paris 8. It brings together computing, disability and new technologies to address digital accessibility and assistive technologies. Apprenticeships are available in the second year (M2).

Courses and projects

Main teaching areas

My teaching combines computing fundamentals, digital accessibility, prototyping and projects developed with external partners.

M1
Web

Accessible Front-End Development

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive design and accessibility principles applied to concrete projects.

M1–M2
IoT

Electronics and Internet of Things

Prototyping, sensors, connected devices and the implementation of technical solutions shaped by user needs.

M1–M2
Cloud

Networks and cloud infrastructures

Network architectures, cloud services and the infrastructure required for digital applications.

Project
fieldwork

Technical projects, internships and work-study

Supervision of projects with partner organisations, internship follow-up and support for apprenticeship assignments.

Teaching approach: real disability-related situations, teamwork, prototyping, autonomy and materials adapted to multidisciplinary cohorts.

Teaching impact

Projects with non-profit and partner organisations

Each year, students develop accessible websites for around 20 non-profit organisations.

From needs to delivery

  1. identify an organisation and its needs
  2. formalise requirements
  3. design and develop accessible websites
  4. test, document and deliver

Work-study and employability

As apprenticeship coordinator, I support approximately 10 to 15 second-year master’s students each year, working with employers on applications, proposed assignments and coordination with the university apprenticeship team.
