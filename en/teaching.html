Teaching

194 equivalent tutorial hours per year, mainly in the MIASHS Master's programme, with projects connected to disability, users and real-world needs.

Overview

Teaching in the MIASHS Master’s programme

The programme is multidisciplinary, bringing together computing, health and the humanities. Content is adapted to varied student backgrounds.

Learning by doing

  • real disability-related situations
  • prototyping and technical projects
  • teamwork and autonomy
  • hybrid learning and digital follow-up tools
  • adaptation to multidisciplinary cohorts

Teaching impact

Projects that help real organisations

Each year, approximately 20 non-profit organisations in France and abroad benefit from an accessible website project delivered by students.

From needs to delivery

  1. identify an organisation and its needs
  2. formalise requirements
  3. design and develop accessibly
  4. test, document and deliver

Work-study and employability

Work-study coordinator for approximately 10 to 15 students per year: employer relations, application support, mission validation and coordination with the university team.
