Anis Rojbi

Academic CV

Université Paris 8 · CHArt research unit, THIM team

Profile

I am an Associate Professor (Maître de conférences) in Computer Science and Engineering at Université Paris 8, a member of the CHArt research unit (THIM team) and Section 61 of the French National Council of Universities (CNU). My research focuses on digital accessibility, signal and image processing, and assistive technologies. Since 2020, I have directed the MIASHS master’s programme and headed the Ingénierie – Cognition – Handicap (ICH) department. I am responsible for the Technology and Disability track. I also coordinate international university partnerships.

Research

I study access to digital content, assistive technologies and ways of analysing physiological signals and human behaviour.

Theme 1 — Image accessibility and multi-level content representations

  • Multigranular image representations that allow visual or tactile exploration at different levels of detail.
  • Applications to web and digital-document access for people with visual impairments.

Theme 2 — Multimodal approaches to fatigue analysis

  • I study approaches to fatigue analysis using visual features and physiological measurements.
  • This work draws on computer vision and machine-learning methods.

Theme 3 — Brain-computer interfaces and physical-action classification

  • With colleagues, I studied how adding noise to electroencephalographic (EEG) recordings affects the classification of phases of a grasp-and-lift movement.
  • The findings concern the Grasp-and-Lift dataset and the classification protocol used in the study.
