Profile

I am an Associate Professor (Maître de conférences) in Computer Science and Engineering at Université Paris 8, a member of the CHArt research unit (THIM team) and Section 61 of the French National Council of Universities (CNU). My research focuses on digital accessibility, signal and image processing, and assistive technologies. I have directed the MIASHS master’s programme since 2020 and headed the Ingénierie – Cognition – Handicap (ICH) department since 2021. I am responsible for the Technology and Disability track. I also coordinate international university partnerships.

Research

My work concerns accessible digital content, assistive technologies, and the analysis of human signals and behaviour.

Theme 1 — Image accessibility and multi-level content representations

  • Multigranular image representations that allow visual or tactile exploration at different levels of detail.
  • Applications to web and digital-document access for people with visual impairments.

Theme 2 — Multimodal approaches to fatigue analysis

  • I study approaches to fatigue analysis using visual features and physiological measurements.
  • This work draws on computer vision and machine-learning methods.

Theme 3 — Brain-computer interfaces and physical-action classification

  • Evaluation of natural and synthetic electroencephalographic (EEG) data augmentation methods for deep-learning-based physical-action classification.
  • Perspectives for human-activity analysis; the findings remain limited to the protocol and data studied.
