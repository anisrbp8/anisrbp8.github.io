Glossary and abbreviations

Definitions of specialist terms used on this website.

IA / AI
Artificial intelligence: computer methods for tasks such as recognising an image.
BCI
Brain–computer interface: a system that uses brain signals to interact with a computer.
EEG
Electroencephalography: measuring electrical activity in the brain.
IHM / HCI
Human–computer interaction: the study of how people use computer systems.
MIASHS
Mathematics and computer science applied to the humanities and social sciences: the French degree programme described on this website.
ICH
Engineering – Cognition – Disability: a department at Université Paris 8.
CHArt
Human and artificial cognition: the name of the research laboratory.
THIM
Refers here to the CHArt research team mentioned in Anis Rojbi’s profile.
CNU
France’s National Council of Universities. Section 61 covers computer engineering, control engineering and signal processing.
UFR
A teaching and research unit within a French university.
CNRS
French National Centre for Scientific Research.
ATER
Temporary teaching and research assistant in a French university.
M1 / M2
First and second years of a master’s degree.
TD / HTD
Tutorials / equivalent tutorial hours: a unit used to measure teaching workload.
IoT
Internet of Things: physical devices connected to a network.
R&D
Research and development.
MSc / PhD
Master of Science / Doctor of Philosophy: academic degrees.
PHC
Hubert Curien Partnership: a programme supporting international research cooperation.
LMD5
LMD means bachelor–master–doctorate. LMD5 refers here to the curriculum structure mentioned in the profile.
HCERES
High Council for the Evaluation of Research and Higher Education in France.
CT / CHSCT
Staff representative bodies: technical committee and committee for health, safety and working conditions, as mentioned in the CV.
ML / deep learning
Machine learning: learning patterns from data. Deep learning: machine learning using artificial neural networks.
Multimodal
Using several kinds of information, such as images and measurements from the body.
Granulation
Breaking information or an image into smaller parts for analysis or a different presentation.
Front-End / cloud
Front end: the part of a website a person interacts with. Cloud: computing services accessed remotely over a network.
Hz / GHz / k€
Hertz / gigahertz: frequency units. k€: thousands of euros.
PDF
Portable Document Format: a downloadable document format.
WCAG / RGAA
Web Content Accessibility Guidelines / France’s general accessibility improvement framework.
IEEE / 5G / SRSS
IEEE: a professional organisation for electronics and computing. 5G: fifth-generation mobile networks. SRSS: Satellite-Routed Sensor System.
PSETH
Science, Engineering, Technology and Healthcare: the subjects of the conference cited in the publications.
