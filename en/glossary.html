Glossary and abbreviations

Definitions of specialist terms used on this website.

AI / IA (French)
Artificial intelligence: methods that allow computer systems to perform tasks such as image recognition, prediction and language processing.
BCI
Brain–computer interface: a system that records and interprets brain signals to enable interaction with a digital device.
EEG
Electroencephalography: a method of recording the brain’s electrical activity, usually using electrodes placed on the scalp.
IHM / HCI
Human–computer interaction: the field concerned with how people interact with digital systems and how those systems are designed.
MIASHS
Mathématiques et informatique appliquées aux sciences humaines et sociales (mathematics and computer science applied to the humanities and social sciences). At Paris 8 this master’s programme includes the Technology and Disability track.
ICH
Engineering – Cognition – Disability: a department at Université Paris 8.
CHArt
Human and artificial cognition: the name of the research laboratory.
THIM
Technologies, Handicaps, Interfaces, Multimodalités: a research team at Université Paris 8 associated with the CHArt laboratory.
CNU
France’s National Council of Universities. Section 61 covers computer engineering, control engineering and signal processing.
UFR
A teaching and research unit within a French university.
CNRS
French National Centre for Scientific Research.
ATER
Temporary teaching and research assistant in a French university.
M1 / M2
First and second years of a master’s degree.
TD / HTD
Tutorials / equivalent tutorial hours: a unit used to measure teaching workload.
IoT
Internet of Things: physical devices connected to a network.
R&D
Research and development.
MSc / PhD
Master of Science / Doctor of Philosophy: academic degrees.
PHC
Hubert Curien Partnership: a programme supporting international research cooperation.
LMD5
LMD means bachelor–master–doctorate. At Paris 8, LMD5 is the name of a curriculum framework for these three degree levels.
HCERES
High Council for the Evaluation of Research and Higher Education in France.
ML / deep learning
Machine learning: methods for fitting models to data. Deep learning: a family of methods based on multilayer neural networks.
Multimodal
Combining several types of data or interaction, such as images, sounds and physiological signals.
Granulation
Organising information into meaningful units (‘granules’), potentially at different levels of detail.
Front-End / cloud
Front end: the part of a website a person interacts with. Cloud: computing services accessed remotely over a network.
Hz / GHz / k€
Hertz / gigahertz: frequency units. k€: thousands of euros.
PDF
Portable Document Format: a file format designed to preserve document layout across devices.
WCAG / RGAA
WCAG: international guidelines for accessible web content. RGAA: the French framework for assessing digital accessibility.
IEEE / 5G / SRSS
IEEE: a professional organisation for electronics and computing. 5G: fifth-generation mobile networks. SRSS: Satellite-Routed Sensor System.
DOI
A Digital Object Identifier is a persistent identifier. In the bibliography, a DOI link can still lead to a paper when the publisher changes its web address.
W3C / WAI
The World Wide Web Consortium publishes web standards. Its Web Accessibility Initiative develops the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines.
dblp
An online bibliography of computer science publications.
